GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Not sure where to start? Tell us what you want to improve, even if you don't know the technical details yet.

Do I need to know what an API is?

No. Explain what you do manually today and what you want to happen automatically. We can explore whether an integration is a good fit.

Can you connect my supplier to Shopify?

Potentially, yes. It depends on the supplier's available API or feed, your Shopify setup, permissions and the data you need to synchronise. We assess those requirements before quoting.

How much does an integration cost?

Pricing depends on complexity, product volume, testing, deployment and ongoing support. After an initial discussion, we can define a scope and provide a quotation.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Often, if those systems provide a supported API, feed, export or another permitted integration method. We'll confirm technical feasibility before committing to the work.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Support and monitoring can be discussed as a separate service. The proposal will state exactly what is included and how additional work is handled.
